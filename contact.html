Frequently Asked Questions

What are the cave timings and closure days for Ajanta and Ellora?

Ajanta Caves: Closed on Mondays. Open 09:00 hrs to 17:00 hrs.
Ellora Caves: Closed on Tuesdays. Open sunrise to sunset.
Our concierge arranges priority ASI tickets, licensed guides, and luxury transport to prevent waiting.

How far is AURA Resort from the airport and railway station?

We are situated on Jalna Road in CIDCO, exactly 5 km (10 minutes) from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Airport (IXU) and 7 km (15 minutes) from Aurangabad Railway Station.

Do you provide chauffeur-driven cars for the caves?

Yes, our fleet includes premium luxury sedans and SUVs with trained chauffeurs familiar with monument routes, best photography spots, and clean highway stops.
