Immerse yourself in UNESCO World Heritage sites, medieval citadels, and centuries of artistic genius right at your doorstep in Aurangabad.

UNESCO Heritage • 28 km

Ellora Caves

The monumental 8th-century monolithic Kailash Temple, carved top-down from a single basalt mountain cliff.

Ancient Art • 102 km

Ajanta Caves

30 rock-cut Buddhist temples dating from 2nd century BCE with world-celebrated ancient mural frescoes.

Mughal Jewel • 6 km

Bibi Ka Maqbara

The famous 'Taj of the Deccan', featuring marble domes, charbagh gardens, and intricate lattice jali stonework.

Medieval Citadel • 16 km

Daulatabad Fort

One of India's most formidable 12th-century hill fortifications with subterranean defense mazes.

Artisanal Craft • 4 km

Himroo & Paithani

Centuries-old royal weaving traditions creating gold-threaded royal Paithani sarees and Persian motifs.

Hotel Policies & Check-In Details +
Check-in & Check-out: Standard check-in is 14:00 hrs and check-out is 12:00 hrs. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability and may incur nominal charges. Government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID) is required at check-in for all adult guests.

Cancellation Policy: Free cancellation up to 48 hours prior to arrival. Cancellations made within 48 hours of check-in date are subject to one night room charge plus applicable taxes.

Children & Extra Beds: Children under 6 years stay complimentary using existing bedding. Extra rollaway beds are available upon advance request.

Heritage Excursions: Private guided tours to Ajanta and Ellora Caves include luxury AC vehicle, authorized Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) guide, monument permits, and gourmet packed refreshments.
