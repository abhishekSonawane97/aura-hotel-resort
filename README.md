# AURA Hotel & Luxury Resort — Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar), Maharashtra

A bespoke, high-fashion editorial website for **AURA Hotel & Luxury Resort**, located near the UNESCO World Heritage Ajanta & Ellora Caves in Aurangabad, Maharashtra, India.

Inspired by the design language and motion aesthetics of [duoswynwood.com](https://duoswynwood.com/), built **100% with pure Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript** with zero external dependencies, build tools, or frameworks.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Pure Vanilla Stack**: Zero frameworks (no React/Vue), zero JS libraries (no jQuery/Swiper), zero CSS frameworks (no Tailwind/Bootstrap). Zero build steps.
- **Cinematic Looping Video Hero**: Full-bleed architectural video background with dynamic dark/light header contrast transitions.
- **Hero Quick Booking Bar**: Instant date pickers, guest selector, and room preference.
- **Regal Deccan Luxury Palette**: Linen Ivory (`#F8F5EE`), Imperial Obsidian (`#162026`), Royal Antique Gold (`#C29B38`), and Polished Sandstone (`#EFE9DC`).
- **Interactive Moving Artworks**: Restored animated line-art motion illustrations (`Man-Luggage-v2-.gif`, `Dancer_2_PINK.gif`, `Palm.gif`, `Avant_Garde_BG.gif`).
- **Meaningful Hospitality SVG Insignias**: Custom AURA heritage crests and 5 authentic hotel accreditation badges.
- **Dynamic Stay Price Calculator**: Live estimation of nights and total price in INR based on selected dates and room category.

---

## 📄 Pages Included

1. **`index.html`** — Master Editorial Homepage (Hero video, booking bar, suite switcher, dining, and Ajanta & Ellora destination slider).
2. **`rooms.html`** — Rooms & Luxury Suites (All 5 room categories, SQ FT specs, inclusions, and nightly rates in INR).
3. **`events.html`** — Royal Weddings, Banquets & Conclaves (Ballrooms, lawns, boardrooms, capacity matrices, and RFP form).
4. **`offers.html`** — Curated Offers & Packages (UNESCO Heritage Discovery, Royal Romance, Ayurvedic Detox Retreat, Long Stay).
5. **`contact.html`** — Contact & Concierge (Airport and railway transit guide, department contacts, interactive inquiry form, and FAQ).

## 🚀 Running Locally

Serve with any simple HTTP server:

```bash
# Python
python3 -m http.server 8765

# Or Node.js
npx serve .
```

Open `http://localhost:8765` in your browser.
